3. Simpan hasil gambar, ubah ukuran ke **600x600 px (Square)** untuk produk atau **1920x800 px** untuk hero banner.
4. Konversi ke format **WebP** (kualitas 85-90%) dan letakkan di folder:

## 1. STYLE GUIDELINES GLOBAL (STANDAR STUDIO PROFESIONAL)
Setiap prompt telah dilengkapi dengan parameter visual:
- **Lighting:** Soft studio lighting with dual softboxes, gentle drop shadow under the product, zero harsh glare.
- **Background:** Clean minimalist off-white / light neutral studio backdrop, slightly blurred warm kitchen or modern grocery pantry in distant background.
- **Style:** Hyper-realistic 8k commercial retail product photography, Indonesian retail packaging design, sharp focus on label details.
